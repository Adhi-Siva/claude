The website should be designed using the uploaded AUM Sol Corp logo and the uploaded AUM IT Training Institute brochure as the primary visual and content references.

====================================================
1. BRAND IDENTITY & DESIGN THEME
====================================================

The entire website must follow the exact visual identity of the AUM Sol Corp logo.

Primary theme:
- Deep navy blue / midnight blue background
- Premium golden/yellow accents
- White typography
- Subtle royal blue and purple gradients where appropriate
- Gold borders, highlights and icons
- Elegant, futuristic but professional IT-training appearance
- Premium corporate education aesthetic

The logo uses a golden AUM emblem on a dark navy background. Preserve this visual identity throughout the website.

Do NOT use a generic education website template.

The website should feel like:
- Premium IT Training Institute
- Professional corporate technology company
- Career-focused learning platform
- Modern online education brand

Use:
- Glassmorphism cards where appropriate
- Soft golden glow effects
- Subtle animated gradients
- Gold hover effects
- Rounded cards
- Elegant shadows
- Smooth transitions
- Professional icons
- Minimal but attractive animations

Avoid excessive animations, excessive gradients, cartoonish designs, or overly bright colors.
